# Cinema cancellation policy

- A cancellation made at least 24 hours before the screening receives a refund of 100% of the
  ticket price.
- A cancellation made at least 2 hours but less than 24 hours before the screening receives a
  refund of 50% of the ticket price.
- A cancellation made less than 2 hours before the screening receives no refund.
- The booking fee is never refundable. Refund percentages apply to the ticket price only.
- Negative prices, fees, or notice periods are invalid.
